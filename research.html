Scientific Agenda

Research Pillars & Methods

Investigating the neural, computational, and behavioral mechanisms that support social adaptability, volition, and interaction.

Pillar 01

Group Influences on Individual Behavior

Human choices and actions are constantly modulated by the presence and behaviors of others. This research program investigates how collective contexts shape individual autonomy and cognitive processing.

Key phenomena under investigation include:

  • • Social and Motor Contagion: How involuntary mimicry and movement entrainment propagate across individuals and artificial avatars.
  • • Conformity & Norm Adaptation: The cognitive and neural costs of conforming to consensus versus maintaining dissenting beliefs.
  • • Sense of Agency in Groups: How diffusion of responsibility and crowd environments alter our subjective feeling of authorship over actions.
Pillar 02

Social Interactions & Joint Action

When two or more individuals interact, they form coupled social systems. We explore how brains, bodies, and artificial agents coordinate seamlessly in real time.

  • • Dyadic Kinematics & Synchrony: Sub-second movement adjustments, gesture dynamics, and interpersonal neural/physiological synchrony.
  • • Human-Artificial Agent Interaction: How humans interact with avatars, virtual agents, and robotic partners compared to fellow humans.
  • • Social Exclusion & Acceptance: The psychological and neural impact of ostracism on collaboration and perceptual sensitivity.
Pillar 03

Intentional Control & Free Will

Our work on intentionality and volition explores the fundamental limits and mechanisms of cognitive control. Can humans choose freely, and what are the neural signatures of deciding to act or abort an action?

  • • Neuro-Cognitive Control: Prefrontal mechanisms supporting deliberate goal maintenance versus automatic response inhibition.
  • • High-Level Beliefs: How explicit philosophical or scientific beliefs regarding determinism and free will modulate moral behavior and punishment.
  • • Cross-Linguistic Moral Judgments: Exploring how concepts of willpower and choice vary across linguistic and cultural communities.
Methodology

Experimental Paradigms & Tools

We leverage a comprehensive suite of neuroimaging, computational, and immersive technologies.

🥽

Virtual Reality (VR)

Fully immersive interactive environments with controlled social avatars, allowing precise manipulation of visual cues and proxemics.

⚡

EEG & Hyperscanning

High temporal-resolution electroencephalography to capture readiness potentials, event-related dynamics, and inter-brain coupling.

👁️

Eye-Tracking & Pupillometry

Wearable and screen-based gaze tracking to quantify joint attention, visual search, and autonomic arousal in interaction.

🔬

fMRI & Neural Imaging

Functional magnetic resonance imaging to resolve localized prefrontal and subcortical networks underlying social decisions.
