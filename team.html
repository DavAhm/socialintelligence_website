Principal Investigator

Prof. Dr. Marcel Brass

Einstein Professor for Social Intelligence • PI in Cluster of Excellence SCIoI

I am a social- and cognitive neuroscientist from Germany. I am Einstein Professor for 'Social Intelligence' at the Berlin School of Mind and Brain and the Department of Psychology at the Humboldt-Universität zu Berlin, and a Principal Investigator in the excellence cluster 'Science of Intelligence'. Previously, I held a research professorship at Ghent University and worked at the Max Planck Institute in Leipzig and Munich. I am interested in the neuro-cognitive mechanisms underlying our social behaviour and group influence, how people intentionally control thoughts and actions, and how high-level beliefs influence basic cognitive processes.
