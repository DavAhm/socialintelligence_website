Opportunities

Join Our Group

We are always interested in passionate and curious researchers to join our lab.

Postdoctoral Researchers

We welcome applications from prospective postdocs with strong backgrounds in cognitive neuroscience, VR, EEG hyperscanning, or computational modeling. We actively support applications for fellowships (Humboldt Foundation, Marie Curie, DFG).

Inquire via Email →

PhD Candidates

Prospective doctoral candidates are typically admitted through graduate programs such as the Berlin School of Mind and Brain, the IMPRS LIFE, or the Science of Intelligence excellence cluster.

Inquire via Email →

Master Theses & Lab Rotations

We regularly supervise Master thesis projects and lab rotations for students enrolled in Psychology, Cognitive Science, Neurobiology, or Mind and Brain at universities in Berlin and abroad.
